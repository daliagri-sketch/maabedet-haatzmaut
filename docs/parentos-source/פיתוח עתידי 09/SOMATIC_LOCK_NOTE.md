## Contract Definition

> A canonical somatic bank is a structured corpus of body-level observations,
> urges, interrupts, inhibitory actions, and language patterns —
> derived from clinical theory and mapped to the 4 locked behavioral patterns
> of מעבדת העצמאות.
>
> It is NOT a phrase bank. NOT a UX component.
> It is the raw clinical substrate from which SOS entries, routine anchors,
> and guidance screen phrases are extracted.
>
> Entries are valid only if they pass 4 tests:
> 1. Physically concrete — not emotional/cognitive
> 2. Specific — clear what the body does
> 3. Executable under load — within 15 seconds
> 4. Does not trigger child alarm or misread as escalation
